**Name** (30 · 27): Yeet – Disk & Cache Cleaner

**Bundle ID:** vn.stevetran.yeet · **SKU:** yeet-macos

**Subtitle** (30 · 30): Clear caches, big files & apps

**Promotional text** (170 · 160):
Developer caches, large and duplicate files, browser data and leftover apps. See exactly what each item is, review it, then clean safely — all on your computer.

**Keywords** (100 · 94):
cleaner,cache,disk,storage,space,duplicate,uninstaller,large files,junk,npm,gradle,deriveddata

**Description** (4000 · 2766):
Yeet shows you what is filling your disk and helps you clean it without guessing.

Caches, forgotten downloads, duplicate copies and the leftovers of apps you removed long ago grow quietly until the disk is full. Yeet finds them in one scan, explains every item in plain words, and lets you review everything before anything is removed.

OVERVIEW
• One dashboard for the whole disk: what is used, what is free and how much can be freed safely
• "Worth cleaning" lists the biggest items from every section, largest first
• A 7-day strip shows how fast your disk is growing

DEVELOPER CACHES
• App build data per project, device support files and simulator caches
• npm, Yarn, pnpm, Bun, CocoaPods, Carthage and other package caches, Gradle, Maven, Python (pip, Poetry, uv), Go, Rust, Flutter & Dart, Homebrew, Claude Code
• Old node_modules and Rust target folders in your projects
• Every item shows its path, size, last change, what deleting affects, and the exact command

SYSTEM JUNK
• App caches, logs and crash reports, installers in Downloads, firmware files and the Trash
• Editor and IDE caches (VS Code, Cursor, older JetBrains versions)

LARGE & OLD FILES
• Files over 50 MB–1 GB in your Home folder, grouped by kind: video, disk images, archives, models, documents
• "Not opened since" filter so forgotten files stand out
• Everything goes to the Trash — nothing is deleted for good

DUPLICATES
• Finds identical files by content, not by name
• Keep the oldest or newest copy; at least one copy is always kept
• Re-checks every file before it is moved

UNINSTALLER
• Removes an app together with its caches, logs, preferences and support files
• Your data (accounts, documents, saved content) is kept unless you tick it
• Shows architecture, size and when each app was last opened

BROWSERS
• Caches and on-device AI models kept by Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc, Vivaldi and Opera
• Passwords, cookies, history, bookmarks, extensions and sign-ins are never touched

STARTUP ITEMS & SYSTEM HEALTH
• See which background agents start with your computer and spot ones whose app is gone
• Battery capacity and cycles, storage and thermal state at a glance

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Nothing is removed until you confirm in the review sheet, which shows what is deleted and what goes to the Trash
• Caches that rebuild themselves are marked Safe; anything that can't be recreated is never listed
• Yeet asks you to quit an app before cleaning its data

PRIVATE
Yeet runs entirely on your computer. It collects nothing and makes no network requests. You choose which folders it may read, and can revoke access at any time in Settings.

Yeet is not affiliated with the makers of the tools and apps it cleans. Product names are used only to identify the files the app supports.

**What’s New** (v2.0):
Yeet 2.0 is a complete redesign.
• New Overview dashboard and a fresh look for every screen
• New: Large & Old Files, Duplicates, Uninstaller, Browsers, Startup Items and System Health
• Review sheet before every clean, with Trash for anything that can't be recreated
• New app icon

- **Không dùng nhãn hiệu Apple** (Xcode, iOS, Mac, macOS, Swift, Safari, Finder…) trong tên, phụ đề, từ khoá, mô tả hay ảnh chụp (đã bị reject theo Guideline 5.2.5 ngày 03/10/2026). Trong app: "App Builds", "System Health", ổ đĩa là "Startup Disk".
- **Support URL:** `https://yeet.221-121-3-132.sslip.io/support.html` · **Privacy Policy URL:** `https://yeet.221-121-3-132.sslip.io/privacy.html`.
- **Privacy:** "Data Not Collected". App không kết nối mạng.
- **App Sandbox:** bản 2.0 chỉ có một cấu hình, luôn sandbox (`Support/YeetAppStore.entitlements`: app-sandbox, user-selected read-write, app-scope bookmarks). Người dùng cấp quyền thư mục Home (bắt buộc) và /Applications (chỉ khi dùng Gỡ ứng dụng) qua NSOpenPanel; quyền được lưu bằng security-scoped bookmark. Không chạy lệnh CLI, không xin quyền admin, không dùng Apple Events.
- **Build để nộp:** workflow `.github/workflows/appstore.yml` (GitHub Actions, macOS 15) tự build mỗi lần push lên `releases/*`. Khi repo có secrets `APPLE_TEAM_ID`, `ASC_KEY_ID`, `ASC_ISSUER_ID`, `ASC_KEY_P8` (API key quyền Admin), workflow archive, ký bằng cloud-managed certificate và xuất `.pkg` (artifact `Yeet-AppStore-pkg`); đặt biến `UPLOAD_TO_ASC=true` để upload thẳng lên App Store Connect. Trên máy Mac: `./scripts/release-appstore.sh`.
- **App Review notes (gợi ý dán vào):**
  "Yeet is a disk and cache cleaner that runs fully inside the App Sandbox with no network access and no data collection. On first launch the user selects their Home folder (user-selected, security-scoped bookmark) so Yeet can measure caches and files. The Uninstaller asks separately for access to /Applications. Nothing is removed until the user confirms a review sheet that lists every item and whether it is deleted (re-creatable caches only) or moved to the Trash (files, duplicates, apps and their leftovers, browser models). Browser passwords, cookies, history and bookmarks are never touched. Startup Items is read-only and links to System Settings › Login Items."
